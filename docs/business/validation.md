# Field validation — the four weekends that decide this

Not a coding document. The goal is one number: **how many PG owners actually hand over money after a tenant moves in.** Everything else is buildable; that behaviour is not controllable, so it gets tested first.

## Step 0 — pick the micro-market

Within 45 minutes of home, dense with IT or college PGs, roughly a 2 km radius. Chennai candidates: OMR (Thoraipakkam, Sholinganallur, Perungudi), Velachery–Guindy, Ambattur, Porur, Tambaram for colleges, T Nagar and Ashok Nagar for working women's hostels.

Search "PG" on Google Maps in that radius and list every result. Expect 60–150. That list is the entire month-one market.

## Weekend 1 — sign 15 owners

> "Sir, I'm Muthu. I'm starting a service that sends verified tenants to PGs in this area. No listing fee, no advance, nothing upfront. I'll take photos of your vacant rooms today, put them online, and send people directly to you for visits. You pay me ₹2,000 only after a tenant actually moves in and pays you. If nobody moves in, you pay nothing. Can I see your vacant beds?"

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "I already pay brokers." | "Brokers take half a month's rent, around ₹5,000. I take ₹2,000, and only after the tenant moves in." |
| "I'm already on NoBroker / Housing." | "Keep them. This costs you nothing unless I fill a bed. Do you get good enquiries from there?" (Listen — that answer is research.) |
| "What if the tenant is bad?" | "You interview them exactly like now. I only send them; you decide." |
| "Why should I trust you?" | "Don't. Pay only after the tenant moves in." |

Target: 15 yeses and 60+ vacant beds photographed. Photo checklist per bed type: room wide shot, bed close-up, bathroom, kitchen or dining, entrance, corridor. Daylight, phone horizontal, no filters.

## Weekend 2 — publish and open the demand tap

A one-page site (published Google Sheet, Notion page, or static HTML), one WhatsApp number on a spare SIM, then post in this order: Chennai subreddit with a genuine "I visited 15 PGs and noted real rents, no brokerage" post; company new-joiner WhatsApp groups via friends in nearby IT offices; college placement cells; one YouTube video from the brother's channel ("How to find a PG in Chennai without paying brokerage") because it keeps ranking; 50 A4 QR posters near metro exits, tea shops, gyms and laundries.

## Weekends 3–4 — be the app, by hand

Reply to every enquiry within 10 minutes, shortlist 3, book the visit, follow up next day. 48 hours after each visit, ask the seeker "did you move in?", then invoice the owner ₹2,000. **That invoice is the entire experiment.**

## Gates

- **GO:** 10+ move-ins arranged and 5+ owners paid without argument.
- **PARTIAL:** move-ins happen but owners haggle or delay → change the model (prepaid credit packs, or a seeker-side fee) before writing more code.
- **KILL:** owners refuse to pay once the tenant is theirs, or seekers prefer a broker they know.

## Budget

Travel ₹2,000–4,000, posters ₹500–1,000, spare SIM ₹300, domain ₹800. Under ₹6,000 total.

## Rules

- File the employer outside-activity disclosure before taking the first rupee.
- Never collect a seeker's deposit or rent.
- Never promise an owner a number of tenants. Promise effort, charge on results.

## What to check in Chennai specifically

1. Empty-bed rate: 30 PGs, total beds and beds empty today.
2. How many PGs a recent joiner called and visited before choosing (ask 15 people).
3. **Will owners use a daily app?** Ask 10 owners how they track rent and vacancy today — register, Excel, WhatsApp, memory. An owner who won't adopt the register can never be Instant Book.
4. Broker presence and fees in that area.
